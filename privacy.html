Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

ACI Weekly Report Automation is a private, single-user tool. This policy describes its intended processing of Google account data.

Information accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the tool accesses Gmail message metadata and Excel attachments relevant to the N8, SBM, and NVCG reports. It also accesses designated Google Drive files and folders needed to read the existing workbook and save updated versions.

Use of information

Information is used only to identify the applicable report batch, consolidate Excel data, preserve meeting notes where applicable, and save dated XLSM outputs. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

Storage and retention

Temporary report attachments and workbooks may be processed within the execution environment. Generated workbooks and historical versions are stored in the Google Drive folder chosen by the account owner until the owner deletes them. OAuth credentials are intended to be stored in restricted-access configuration or secret storage, not published on this website.

Sharing

The tool is not designed to sell or share Google user data with advertisers or data brokers. Processing may involve the Google APIs and the cloud infrastructure configured by the account owner.

Security and control

Access is limited to the account owner and the permissions granted through Google OAuth. The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account connections settings, and can delete stored output files from Google Drive.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact wandangle@gmail.com.

This page should be updated if the application's actual data handling, storage, or users change.
